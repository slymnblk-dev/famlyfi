Privacy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: your financial data is yours. We only store it so the app works for you, we never sell it, and there are no ads or trackers.

What we store

  • Your email address, if you create an account, so you can sign in.
  • What you enter in the app: categories, entries, accounts, balances and goals.
  • App settings like theme, currency and your name.

Where it's stored

  • Without an account: only in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • With an account: in a database hosted by Supabase in Zurich, Switzerland, so it can sync between your devices. Each account can only ever read its own data.
  • The website is served by Netlify.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't connect to your bank. Everything is entered by you.

Your choices

  • You can use Famlyfi without an account.
  • You can erase all data on a device anytime in Settings → Erase everything.
  • To delete your account and all its data permanently, email us at privacy@famlyfi.com from the address you sign in with.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@famlyfi.com
